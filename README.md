### 🏛️ Production Systems & Real-World Impact

#### 🏢 BITS Telepresence & Polycom Booking System (TPBooking)
> **Adopted by the Director, Vice Chancellor, Deans, and 100+ Senior Faculty across 4 BITS Campuses (Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad, Dubai).**
- Architected an enterprise multi-campus reservation and governance platform on **ASP.NET (.NET 4.8) and Microsoft SQL Server**.
- Implemented **Google OAuth 2.0 JWT validation**, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 administrative access control, automated notification pipelines, and transactional conflict-free booking APIs.
- Digitize and manage critical institutional telepresence suites with real-time room availability status.

#### 🔒 NTS Portal — Secure Nomination Management System
> **Adopted by 25+ Department HoDs across BITS Pilani; replaced manual workflows and cut administrative time by 90%.**
- Engineered a role-based ASP.NET nomination workflow with CAPTCHA validation, login-throttling authentication, and trusted-IP verification.
- Built atomic draft/final submission transactional workflows, audit logging, and department-wise administration dashboards.

#### 📱 Flowpay — Payments Without Internet
> **Dual-rail offline UPI payment architecture designed for real-world connectivity constraints in India.**
- Implemented dual-rail architecture: **`*99#` USSD** for legacy GSM signaling and **UPI 123Pay (IVR) DTMF** for VoLTE/all-IP networks (such as Jio) that do not support USSD.
- Pure client-side parsing of bank transaction confirmations without third-party server intermediaries or sensitive credential retention.

#### 🔬 ChrominoSense — Mobile Spectrometer Engine
> **Scientific computing research project under Dr. I. R. Laskar (HoD, Chemistry, BITS Pilani).**
- Developed an optical calibration pipeline estimating spectral wavelengths directly from smartphone camera RGB data within a **~5 nm margin of error**.
- Built a streamlined analytical interface delivering spectral capture and analysis in under 2 seconds.

#### 📄 Research Trust Report
> **Academic paper transparency and provenance audit platform.**
- Automated verification system analyzing paper DOIs to extract author funding disclosures, journal provenance, citation anomalies, and retraction indices.
